What matters

There is no VSL. The proof <i>is</i> the pitch

Every video embedded across the funnel is a customer interview — 12 of them, totalling 6 hours 9 minutes, the longest running 87 minutes. There is no presenter-led sales video anywhere. He has replaced the argument with the evidence and let the prospect choose how much of it to consume.

Every title is a name, a number and a place

“@nguyensteadycutting makes $20k/m as a barber in Richmond Virginia”. “Barber makes $20k/m cutting in parents garage”. “@terrance90_ went from $1.6k to $6.9k/m in 60 days”. Handle, figure, and a specific humble setting. For a barber earning $2k in a rented chair, the garage detail is the proof — not the $20k. The setting does the identification work the number cannot.

Long-form proof is a filter, not just proof

Nobody watches 87 minutes of another barber's interview unless they are seriously considering this. The runtime pre-qualifies intent before the call, at zero cost to him. Compare Viral Coach, which uses twelve shallow proof cards to kill a breadth objection — opposite tactic, opposite problem. Dlucs sells one niche deeply; Viral Coach sells every niche shallowly. Each built the proof shape their objection required.

The two-step is labelled on the page

“Step 1 of 2: Watch Video” and “Step 2 of 2: Book Your Call”. Naming the number of steps caps the perceived commitment. A prospect who knows there are exactly two will start; one who cannot see the end of the process often will not.

Browser push notifications are installed

PushCrew runs alongside ClickFunnels, Calendly, Wistia, Intercom, Hyros, Meta Pixel and GTM. A retargeting channel that survives ad-blockers and does not need an email address. Nobody else in this file runs it.
